## What Meitheal is

A platform for de-bottlenecking art collectives. It spreads the administrative
and organisational work that usually lands on one or two overloaded people
(memberships, bookings, funding, communication, publishing) across the whole
collective. Built for NCF, the North Connacht Co-op, an arts cooperative in
Ballina, Co. Mayo (https://thencf.art).

## Who it serves

- **Members**: join, pay, book shared spaces, find funding, submit proposals,
  appear in the artist directory if they choose.
- **Editors**: publish events and posts, confirm bookings.
- **Owners**: approve members, run payments and the box office, oversee the site.
- **The public**: see what is on, read the news, sign up to the newsletter,
  buy tickets, contact the co-op.

## Principles

- The measure of success is how much admin work moves off the few people who
  carry it today.
- Equal access: any member can add a funding opportunity, so access to
  opportunities does not depend on who is in the right inbox.
- Members' money and personal data are handled conservatively. Payments,
  emails to members, sign-in and roles change only with the owner's say-so.
- Plain and boring wins: Rails conventions, small steps, no clever machinery.
